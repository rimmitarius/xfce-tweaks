# Source baseline

Pristine upstream xfdesktop 4.21.0.

Official archive: https://archive.xfce.org/src/xfce/xfdesktop/4.21/xfdesktop-4.21.0.tar.xz

The bundled archive was downloaded and compared byte-for-byte with this official file. Archive hashes are in SHA256SUMS. The pristine archive is the project's baseline commit, `c492a6af7626ecfea64197aaca3fce7248f1dca0`.

Private dependency archives: libxfce4ui 4.21.0 and libxfce4windowing 4.20.7, retained from the earlier source package. Their original license notices are preserved in the archives.
